Why SGD Bounces in Valley

The Valley has formula f(x,y) = x² + 50y². The gradient in y is 50x larger than in x.

SGD takes step: w = w - lr × grad

Big y-gradient → big y-step → overshoots → bounces back!

Why Adam Works

Adam divides by √v where v ≈ grad². This normalizes the step size:

step ≈ grad / |grad| = ±1

Large gradients get divided by large values → controlled steps!
